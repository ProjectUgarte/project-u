Why SpeedBreaker

Better than you remember it

The golden-hour haze. The Blacklist. Pursuits that never let up, and every live-action cutscene. It's all here, the way the Xbox 360 drew it, with a few modern comforts on top.

  • Up to 60 fps

  • Every screen shape

    Ultrawide shows more road; a 4:3 iPad, more above and below. Nothing stretched.

  • Sharp, and true to the original

    Up to 4K, with the original colours, reflections and low-sun glare.

    Steam Deck, Steam Machine, Steam Frame, Linux PCs and Macs, plus iPhone and iPad.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is this an official EA product?

No. SpeedBreaker is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Electronic Arts or the Need for Speed brand.

Does it come with the game?

No. You need a backup of your own Xbox 360 disc. SpeedBreaker doesn't include, provide or link to game files.

Which copy of the game do I need?

The USA Xbox 360 disc (English). The installer checks your copy and tells you if it's a version it doesn't support.

Is it an emulator?

No. It's a static recompilation: the game's Xbox 360 code was translated ahead of time into a native app for your device, so that code runs directly instead of being emulated.

Can it run above 60 fps?

Not yet. Most Wanted's physics, AI and timers are built around 60 steps a second, so simply running it faster changes how the game plays. Higher frame rates (120 Hz and up) are being investigated in a way that keeps the gameplay exactly as it is.

My Mac says it can't open SpeedBreaker.

The Mac app isn't notarized by Apple yet. Try to open it once, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway and confirm with your password. You only need to do this once per download. SpeedBreaker needs macOS 26 or later.

Does it run on Intel Macs?

Not right now. The Mac download needs Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26 or later. An Intel Mac version may come later, but there's no Intel Mac to test it on yet. On x86 PCs, SpeedBreaker needs a processor with AVX2: Intel Haswell (2013) or newer, or AMD Excavator or Zen.

How do I get it on iPhone or iPad?

You build it yourself with Xcode on a Mac and install it on your own device. There's no download, and it isn't on the App Store. It needs iOS 17 or later (iPhone 13 or newer, or an M‑series iPad) and a controller or keyboard. How it works

Why are there thin bars on my Steam Deck?

The Deck's screen is a little taller than the game, so you get thin bars instead of a stretched picture. On a Deck OLED, set the screen to 60 Hz for smooth motion.

Is it VR on the Steam Frame?

No. It plays on a big flat screen in the headset. For the smoothest ride there, set Display › Frame Rate to 30 fps. The game keeps its full speed.

Are my saves kept when I update?

Yes. Saves and settings live apart from the game, so a newer SpeedBreaker keeps them.

What about Windows and Android?

Both are coming. No dates yet.
