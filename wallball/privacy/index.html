WallBall Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

WallBall is a single-player puzzle game designed with privacy as a default. The app operates entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. WallBall does not require an account, does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or any other device data, and does not transmit any information from your device to us or any third party.

The app stores the following data locally on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults):

  • Leaderboard entries — When you complete a game, the app stores your top scores along with the 6-character display name you enter (if any) and the level reached. This data is saved only on your device and is never transmitted off it.
  • Game state — Current level, score, and lives during a session. This is held in memory and is not persisted between app launches.

If you delete the WallBall app, all of this locally-stored data is removed from your device.

How We Use Information

We do not use your information because we do not collect it. Locally-stored leaderboard data is used solely to display your scores in the in-app leaderboard.

Information Sharing

We do not share any information because we do not collect any. No data is sold, rented, transmitted, or shared with third parties, partners, advertisers, or any other party.

Third-Party Services

WallBall does not integrate with third-party analytics services, advertising networks, social media SDKs, or any other external services. The app contains no embedded trackers, no remote API calls, and no network communication of any kind.

WallBall is distributed through the Apple App Store. Apple's collection and use of data when you download or use apps from the App Store is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, separate from ours.

Data Storage & Security

All app data (leaderboard entries, settings) is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage mechanism. No data is sent to remote servers because the app makes no network requests.

iOS handles encryption and security of on-device data according to Apple's platform-level protections.

Children's Privacy

WallBall is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no chat features, no user-generated content sharing, and no external links.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions, please contact us at the address below.

Your Choices

Because WallBall does not collect or transmit any data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. To remove all locally-stored data (including your leaderboard scores), simply delete the app from your device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make changes to this policy in the future — for example, if we add features that change what data the app handles — we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and update the policy accordingly. Material changes will be reflected in a new app version's release notes.
