About

A retro-inspired puzzle game based on the 1992 classic JezzBall. Build walls to trap bouncing balls and fill 75% of the field to advance — but don't let them hit your walls as they generate. Quick reflexes and smart positioning are key. Originally a browser game, now ported as a native iOS app with a local leaderboard, multiple lives, and a clock-based bonus on every level.
